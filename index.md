## Why Jewelry Stores Need Automated Pricing

Gold and silver prices change frequently. For jewelry stores with hundreds or thousands of products, manually updating every variant is time-consuming and prone to pricing errors.

A typical jewelry product may have pricing based on:

- Gold or silver weight
- Metal purity (24K, 22K, 18K, 14K)
- Current live metal rate
- Making charges
- Diamond or gemstone weight
- Minimum selling price / margin requirements

JewelSync lets you configure these rules once and apply them across your entire Shopify catalog.

## How It Works

1. **Create Your Formula** — Define how prices should be calculated for each product or collection.
2. **Set Your Variables** — Assign metal weight, purity, making charge and other values to your variants.
3. **Run the Update** — JewelSync processes your catalog and pushes updated prices to Shopify.
