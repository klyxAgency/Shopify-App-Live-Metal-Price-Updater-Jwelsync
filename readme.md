# JewelSync — Shopify Jewelry Pricing Automation

**Automatically update gold, silver, platinum and precious metal jewelry prices on Shopify based on changing market rates.**

JewelSync is a Shopify pricing automation app built for **jewelry stores, gold merchants, silver retailers and precious metal businesses** that need to keep product and variant prices updated without manually editing thousands of Shopify products.

Instead of maintaining spreadsheets or manually changing prices whenever metal rates move, JewelSync lets Shopify merchants create pricing formulas based on **metal rate, weight, purity, making charges and other product variables**.

## Why Jewelry Stores Need Automated Pricing

Gold and silver prices can change frequently. For jewelry stores with hundreds or thousands of products, manually updating every variant can be time-consuming and prone to pricing errors.

A typical jewelry product may have pricing based on:

- Gold or silver weight
- Metal purity
- Current metal rate
- Making charges
- Diamond or gemstone weight
- Product-specific pricing rules
- Minimum selling price
- Margin requirements

JewelSync allows these pricing rules to be configured once and applied across your entire Shopify catalog.

### Automatic Gold Price Updates

Automatically update Shopify jewelry prices based on gold rates.

Supported gold purities include:

- 22K Gold

- 14K Gold

Use gold weight and purity as variables inside your pricing formula.


### Bulk Update Shopify Product Variants

Updating thousands of jewelry variants manually can take hours.

JewelSync uses Shopify's bulk processing capabilities to update large numbers of variants efficiently.

This is useful for Shopify stores with:

- Hundreds of products
- Thousands of variants
- Large jewelry catalogs
- Frequently changing metal prices

---

### Custom Jewelry Pricing Formula Builder

Create pricing formulas based on your own jewelry business rules.

For example:

```
(Metal Rate + Making Charge) × Metal Weight
```

Or:

```
(Metal Rate × Weight) + Making Charge + Additional Cost
```

Your pricing formula can be configured to match the way your business calculates jewelry prices.

---

### Stop-Loss Price Protection

Metal prices can move quickly. A pricing formula that directly follows market rates may sometimes produce prices below your desired selling price.

JewelSync provides price floor / stop-loss protection so merchants can define minimum acceptable prices.

This can help protect:

- Product margins
- Minimum selling prices
- Wholesale pricing
- Promotional pricing
- Business-specific pricing rules

Suppose a jewelry product contains:
